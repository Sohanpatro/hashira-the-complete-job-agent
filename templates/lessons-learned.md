# 09 Lessons Learned

**Purpose:** one running doc of everything learned from each application, interview and outcome, so no mistake happens twice. Hashira reads **Part 1** before every application, recruiter call and interview round, and adds to it after every outcome (pass or fail). The Tracker keeps only a one-line Journal note that points here.

## Part 1: Standing rules (read before every call)

Distilled from the entries in Part 3. Each rule names its source. *Unconfirmed* means a likely cause, not yet confirmed by feedback.

### Screening and recruiter calls
- *(example)* Work authorization: qualify early and answer directly and honestly. (Company, Month Year, unconfirmed)
- *(example)* Pay: don't name a number first at the screen; ask for the band. (Company, Month Year)

### Recruiters and referrals
- *(example)* Recruiter-sourced roles at large companies: reply to the recruiter first with interest and ask about next steps; don't apply through the links they send. (Coach, Month Year)
- *(example)* Don't hold a suitable application for a referral; ask in parallel. (Coach, Month Year)

### Positioning
- *(example)* Bring up shipped AI work in the intro before you're asked. (Company, Month Year)

### Job search and alerts
- *(example)* Lean relaxed on filters so no viable role is missed; sort afterwards.

## Part 2: Rejection debrief protocol (reusable, every company)

1. **Stay calm and read the email.** Template or personal? How fast did it come? At what stage? Who decided?
2. **List possible causes** from the call notes, most likely first, and mark each as controllable or not.
3. **Ask the candidate the debrief questions:** any reactions on the call (visa, pay, location, level, timeline)? Which questions were asked? Anything that felt off?
4. **Reply within 24 hours:** thank them, ask for one line of feedback, keep the door open (template in Part 4). The candidate approves before anything is sent.
5. **Log the outcome:** add a Part 3 entry, distill any new rule into Part 1, set the Pipeline stage, add a one-line Journal lesson pointing here.
6. **Set a revisit date** if the company invites it.
7. **When feedback arrives,** update the entry and confirm or remove the "unconfirmed" rules.
