# Changelog

## v1.1.0 (2026-11-23)
- Resumes in LaTeX: master and tailored resumes are a .tex with its compiled .pdf (single column, clickable links, ATS-readable text, no hanging one- or two-word lines); new `templates/resume-template.tex`. Google Doc fallback kept.
- Lessons Learned doc: new `{{LESSONS_DOC_ID}}` placeholder and `templates/lessons-learned.md` (standing rules, reusable rejection debrief protocol, entries, templates). Skills read Part 1 before every application, recruiter reply and interview.
- Optional backup track (for example SDE) alongside the AI FDE track, with a loose prep split and an application mix that follows the market.
- New search rules: reply to recruiters at large companies before applying through their links; run referral asks in parallel with applications; keep an optional career coach informed.
- Lead skill: an URGENT block of overdue, due-soon and stale Pipeline rows opens every standup and close.
- Prep skill: reminders before and after every real interview, including telling the coach two days ahead.
